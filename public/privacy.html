Play without an account. Your game records and settings stay on your device. The current game has no advertising, analytics SDKs or in-app purchases.

Who we are

TapeeTepee openstudio operates TAPtoTEST. This policy covers the Android game, its web version and support enquiries. Privacy and support contact: wnsdydtml@gmail.com.

Game information on your device

We save music, sound and vibration preferences and best results for PUZZLE, PORTRAIT and POSITION. A result may include the game and picture identifier, completion status, time, score, mistakes, number of matches and stage reached. An existing tutorial-completion setting is also preserved. The current game does not save an unfinished board for later resumption.

Android uses app-private preferences storage through Capacitor Preferences. The web version uses browser localStorage. When available, older localStorage records from the same Android app are copied once into app preferences without deleting the originals. We keep a local recovery copy of saved values. The game does not transmit these records to a developer-operated server or share them with advertising or analytics services.

The game does not ask for your name or date of birth, or request location, contacts, camera or microphone access. There are no accounts, chat, public profiles, player uploads or cloud record synchronisation.

Android, Google Play and web hosting

Android backup is enabled. Your operating system may back up or restore app data depending on your device and Google account settings. We do not operate or access that service and cannot guarantee restoration. Google Play and the operating system handle store, security and diagnostic information under their own policies and settings.

The installed Android game includes its pictures, music, videos, fonts and policy documents locally. Visiting the web version or this policy online involves our hosting provider, Vercel. It receives information needed to deliver pages, such as IP address, request details and browser information, and may retain technical logs for delivery and security. This differs from playing the bundled Android game. We do not add advertising cookies, analytics tracking or remote fonts to the game or this policy.

Support email

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments. We use these to answer your request and resolve support or privacy issues. Our email provider is Google Gmail. Please send only what is needed for your enquiry, not passwords or sensitive personal details.

We do not sell personal information or use support messages for advertising. Hosting and email providers process information as needed to provide their services. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

Children and families

Children can play without providing personal information. The game does not use behavioural advertising, advertising identifiers or tracking SDKs. Parents or guardians should contact us on a child's behalf. If we learn that a child has sent personal information that we do not need, we will delete it unless retention is legally required.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local records and recovery copies remain until you clear the app's storage or this site's browser data, remove the app, or the platform removes them. Platform backups may exist separately. Updates using the same app identity and storage keys are designed to retain records, but device storage, backup and restore are not guaranteed. Uninstalling, clearing data or changing phones can lose records. Manage platform backups through your device or Google account settings.

The TAPtoTEST Android app uses the package identifier io.github.junyyyong.taptotest. Its private records are specific to this app and are not automatically transferred from a different app. Web records are tied to their browser profile and website address; records from another domain are not automatically transferred.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as needed to handle the enquiry or meet legal obligations, then deleted. To request access, correction or deletion of information you sent us, contact wnsdydtml@gmail.com. We cannot remotely access or erase records stored only on your device. There is no game account to delete.

Security and changes

Android preferences are held in the app's private storage; web records are held in the browser's site storage. Protect your device and email account. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. If data practices change, we will update this policy and the date above.
